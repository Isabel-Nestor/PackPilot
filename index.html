FAQs

Good questions, simple answers.

Does PackPilot replace an ERP?

No. The proposed product focuses on sales visibility, reminders and customer updates alongside existing systems.

Can customers see other companies' orders?

The demo preview filters orders to the selected customer. Secure real-user authentication and server-side access control are planned, not implemented.

Does the demo send real messages or take payments?

No. Nudges, customer messages and payments are simulated and saved only in the browser.

Is this ready to purchase?

Not yet. PackPilot is a concept prototype with illustrative pricing.
