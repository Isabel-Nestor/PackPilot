Who PackPilot is for

Purpose-built for packaging sales representatives.

Designed for Irish companies supplying bespoke printed packaging, including branded bags, cartons and retail packaging.

Daily users

Sales representatives and purchasing staff manage orders; customers view their own updates and request repeat quotes.

Decision-makers

Managing directors and financial controllers decide whether to introduce the software.

Who pays

The packaging supplier pays a company subscription; customer access is included.

The existing landscape
