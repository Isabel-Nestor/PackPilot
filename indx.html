01 / The problem

Less chasing. More doing.

Customers often rely on sales representatives for updates, while representatives chase purchasing, payments, production milestones and freight movements across multiple orders.

Who needs it?

Specific segment: Sales representatives at Irish bespoke packaging suppliers coordinating overseas custom-printed orders, and their retail customers ordering branded bags and boxes.

Buyer: Packaging supplier management. Daily users: sales representatives and customers.

In their words

“I need to know where my packaging is, what's left to pay and when it will arrive, without having to email for every update.”

Invented customer quote for illustration.

“I want to spot overdue deposits and shipping delays before customers start chasing me.”

Invented sales representative quote.

What you can do with PackPilot

Current approach: a mixture of emails, phone calls, purchasing updates and internal order systems. This is a fictional concept informed by a typical bespoke packaging workflow, not a claim about any particular company.

02 / The solution

Simple from start to finish.

📋

1. Record

Staff create an order with its milestones, shipping method, deposit, balance and credit terms.

🚢

2. Track

Staff update production and transport stages. The system flags delays and outstanding payment actions.

💬

3. Respond

Customers see approved updates, supply delivery details and request quotes on previous or new products.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions. Clear answers.

Is PackPilot a real commercial service?

No. This is a student-built, interactive concept prototype.

Can customers see other customers' orders?

The demo filters orders by a selected fictional customer. Real account authentication and secure access would need to be built before launch.

Where do shipping updates come from?

In this prototype, staff enter them manually. Live factory, carrier and ERP integrations are planned features, not implemented.

Will my changes remain after refreshing?

Yes, in the same browser using localStorage. They do not sync across devices.
